# Overdraft (Northwind Bank, fictional)

## Overdraft fee
If a chequing account balance goes below zero, an overdraft fee of $15 is charged per day, for a maximum of 3 days per overdraft period.

## Interest
Overdrawn balances are charged interest at 19.9% per year, calculated daily.

## How to avoid it
Customers can turn on low-balance alerts in the mobile app, or link their savings account for automatic transfers to cover the shortfall.
