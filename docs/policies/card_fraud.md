# Lost, Stolen or Fraudulent Card (Northwind Bank, fictional)

## Immediate steps
If a card is lost or stolen, freeze it immediately in the mobile app under Cards, then Freeze card. A frozen card can be unfrozen if it is found. If the card is not found, request a replacement; it arrives in 5 to 7 business days.

## Liability
The customer has zero liability for fraudulent transactions reported within 2 days of the card being lost. After 2 days, liability is limited to $50 for transactions the customer did not authorise.

## Suspected fraud on the account
If the customer sees a transaction they do not recognise, they should report it right away so a dispute ticket can be opened and the card replaced.
