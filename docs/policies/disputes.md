# Transaction Disputes (Northwind Bank, fictional)

## Time limit
A customer must report a disputed card transaction within 60 days of the transaction date. Disputes reported after 60 days may be declined.

## What counts as a dispute
Valid reasons include a duplicate charge, a charge for goods or services never received, an incorrect amount, and a transaction the customer did not authorise.

## How a dispute is handled
To open a dispute we need the transaction ID and a short reason. Once a dispute ticket is opened, a provisional credit is applied within 5 business days for disputed amounts under $500. The investigation can take up to 45 days. The customer is told the outcome by secure message.

## What a dispute is not
A dispute is not a refund request for a purchase the customer simply regrets. Contact the merchant first for refunds.
