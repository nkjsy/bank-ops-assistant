# Transfers and Payments (Northwind Bank, fictional)

## Daily limits
The default online transfer limit is $5,000 per day across all accounts. Limits can be raised temporarily by calling the bank, after identity verification.

## Cut-off times
Transfers submitted before 5pm on a business day are processed the same day. Transfers submitted after 5pm, or on weekends and public holidays, are processed the next business day.

## International wire transfers
An international wire transfer costs a flat fee of $25. Wires usually arrive within 1 to 3 business days.
